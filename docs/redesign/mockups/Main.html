Deep Learning Lab · design language

Activation

The course is a network, and you light it up. Every lesson you master switches on a neuron, so colour on this site means one thing only: how much you have learned.

Principles
  1. The labs are the signature. Everything else gets quieter so they can be loud.
  2. One meaning per colour. Viridis for progress, blue and orange for data, green and red only for right and wrong.
  3. Chrome gets out of the way. One navigation, no floating buttons, nothing over the text.
  4. Every page answers “what next?” before it shows anything else.
